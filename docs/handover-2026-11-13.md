## The open problem: the client half of networked entities

The client makes its copies of networked entities from the network (ClientSpawnEntity_createFromNetwork ->
0x143469110 -> FUN_1431bf650 / FUN_1431bf530), resolving each ghost's **parent bus by a network id**. Our spawn's
buses have no id the client knows, so the first ghost crashes the client thread (FUN_14348db6e / 0x14314b410 on
null). Tried, in order:

1. Spawn our client half ourselves: never reached; the network ghost crashes first.
2. Network container borrowed from a live dash: the client character went and the level ended (do not reuse).
3. Our own container: server fine, client crash on the ghost (as above).
4. Leaving out CharacterProxyData: it is not a top-level object of the dash (0 left out); not the cause anyway.
5. StreamRealm 2 on our reference: the game makes a ServerBlueprintEntity; event `0xd27bc17e` (stream in:
   networked-prefab-spawn c5) streams the dash in on the server; the client then crashed resolving the parent
   bus of the first ghost (FUN_14348d990 returned null). Our **outer** blueprint's bus is not known to the client.

Next step: the research task **`bus-network-ids`** (brief and plan on the PC, `.workflow/runs/bus-network-ids/`):
how a bus gets the id the client resolves; whether a ServerBlueprintEntity can be spawned under a bus the client
already knows with our links inside the blueprint it streams in (no outer bus); whether a spawn can be local only.
